Chirayu Kothari

Ph.D. Candidate in Civil & Environmental Engineering at University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign

I conduct research at the interface of cement chemistry, optical spectroscopy, and sustainable construction materials in the Garg Research Group advised by Prof. Nishant Garg. My work combines confocal Raman chemical imaging, X-ray diffraction, and chemo-physical modeling to accelerate the decarbonization and long-term durability of infrastructure materials.

Core Research Thrusts

Explore Detailed Methodology →
01 / SPECTROSCOPY & CHARACTERIZATION

Quantitative Phase Analysis of Anhydrous Cements

Coupling confocal Raman imaging with X-ray diffraction (XRD) Rietveld analysis to resolve multi-phase clinker mineralogies in Portland (OPC) and Calcium Sulfoaluminate (CSA) cements.

Cement & Concrete Research (2024) →
02 / SUSTAINABILITY & INNOVATION

UR²: 5-Minute Reactivity Test for SCMs & Clays

Developed an ultra-rapid chemical dissolution test measuring active Si & Al in 5 minutes at 90°C, offering real-time plant quality control for calcined clays and low-carbon LC³ binders.

Cement & Concrete Research (2025) →
03 / GEOTECHNICS & PAVEMENTS

Quarry By-Products for Pavement Foundations

Investigating the long-term cementation and hydrotalcite-mediated freeze-thaw durability of dolomitic aggregate by-products, monitored in real time via ultrasonic shear wave sensors.

Transportation Geotechnics (2025) →
04 / THERMODYNAMIC MODELING

Chemo-Thermal-Hygral Modeling of DEF Kinetics

Numerical modeling of heat-cured concrete structures to predict Delayed Ettringite Formation (DEF) kinetics, internal sulfate sorption, and long-term expansion behavior.

Constr. & Build. Materials (2022) →
